================================================================================
                            RELATIONSHIP SUMMARY
================================================================================

1. PRODUCTS → PRODUCT_SPECIFICATIONS (One-to-Many)
   - One product can have multiple specifications
   - Cascade delete: Deleting a product deletes its specifications

2. PRODUCTS → PRODUCT_IMAGES (One-to-Many)
   - One product can have multiple images (main, raw, fried, gallery)
   - Cascade delete: Deleting a product deletes its images

3. PRODUCTS ↔ SUITABILITY_TAGS (Many-to-Many)
   - Junction table: product_suitability_tags
   - One product can have multiple tags (Vegan, Halal, etc.)
   - One tag can be associated with multiple products
   - Cascade delete: Deleting a product removes its tag associations

4. PROCESS_STEPS → PROCESS_STEP_POINTS (One-to-Many)
   - One process step can have multiple bullet points
   - Cascade delete: Deleting a step deletes its points

5. INDEPENDENT TABLES (No Foreign Keys)
   - certifications
   - partners
   - contact_inquiries
   - company_info (single record)
   - company_stats


================================================================================
                            INDEXES SUMMARY
================================================================================

All tables have indexes on:
- Primary Keys (automatic)
- Foreign Keys
- Frequently queried columns (category, type, status, is_active, display_order)
- Unique columns (product_code, slug, email_address)


================================================================================
                            DATA TYPES SUMMARY
================================================================================

- IDs: INT/SERIAL (Auto-increment)
- Text: VARCHAR(255-500) for short text, TEXT for long content
- Dates: DATE for dates, TIMESTAMP for date-time
- Booleans: BOOLEAN (is_active, etc.)
- Enums: ENUM types for status, image_type (MySQL) or custom types (PostgreSQL)
